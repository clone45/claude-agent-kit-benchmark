1. **Press → dispatch.** `onButton` (`src/modules/PianoRoll/PianoRollEditorWidget.hpp:1624`) handles a left press by calling `beginDrag` (`:1889`). `beginDrag` routes grid-zone presses (decided by `zoneAt`, `:1387`) to `beginGridDrag` (`:2032`), which makes the decision.
2. **Setup in `beginGridDrag`.** It first calls `noteIndexAt(step, pitch)` (`:1406`) to find the note body under the pointer. That test uses the half-open span `start <= step < end`, so a note's end step is not inside it. It then calls `selectionBounds` (`:1455`) to get the group box, which only exists when two or more notes are selected.
3. **Order of checks, first match wins:**
   - **A. Shift held** (`:2047`): toggles the note's selection, or starts a marquee.
   - **B. Pointer inside the selection box** (`:2062`): always a move (`beginMove` / `beginMoveSelection`). Edges are never tested here, so a note inside a group can't be resized.
   - **C. `edgeGrabAt(position, grab)`** (`:2078`): sets `DRAG_RESIZE`, and `drag_left_edge = grab.left_edge` picks which edge.
   - **D. `index >= 0`** (`:2095`): `selectOnly` + `beginMove`, which sets `DRAG_MOVE`.
   - E/F: a click on empty space clears the selection or paints a new note.
4. **Grab zone, `edgeGrabAt`** (`:1422-1451`). For each note on the pointer's pitch, from topmost down, it computes pixel x0/x1 and `zone = min(EDGE_GRAB_W, max(1, (x1-x0)/3))` (`:1439`). `EDGE_GRAB_W` is 4 px (`PianoRollGeometry.hpp:127`).
   - Notes 12 px wide or more get the full ±4 px.
   - Notes 3–12 px wide get a third of their width.
   - Notes under 3 px get ±1 px.
   - The shrinking keeps a fixed zone from covering a narrow note completely, which would make it impossible to move.
5. **Right edge before left.** The right edge is tested first and returns at once (`:1441`). The zone is measured in pixels around x1, so it reaches past the note's end. This is the only way to grab the right edge, since `noteIndexAt` never counts x1 as inside the note.
6. **Why short notes have no left edge.** The left test (`:1445`) also requires `(x1-x0) > EDGE_GRAB_MIN_NOTE_W`, which is `EDGE_GRAB_W * 2.5`, or 10 px (`PianoRollGeometry.hpp:128`). On a note 10 px wide or less, the two edges are so close that a left grab could be mistaken for a right grab (comment at `:1443`). So a press near its left edge falls through to D and moves the note.
7. `onHover` (`:1599`) calls the same `edgeGrabAt` to show the resize cursor, so the cursor matches what a press will do.
