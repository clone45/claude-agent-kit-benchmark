All paths are in `src/modules/PianoRoll/PianoRollEditorWidget.hpp` (PREW) unless noted.

1. **Call path:** `onButton` (PREW:1624) passes a left-button press to `beginDrag` (PREW:1890). `beginDrag` calls `zoneAt` (PREW:1387), and for `ZONE_GRID` it calls `beginGridDrag` (PREW:2032).
2. **Checks inside `beginGridDrag`, in order:** it first calls `noteIndexAt(step, pitch)` (PREW:1408) to find the note under the pointer and `selectionBounds` to get the selection box. Then:
   - A (2046): Shift held → toggle the note's selection, or start a marquee.
   - B (2060): pointer inside the multi-note selection box → move. Edges are never tested here, so a note in a group can't be resized.
   - C (2076): `edgeGrabAt(position, grab)` (PREW:1422) → `DRAG_RESIZE`, with `drag_left_edge = grab.left_edge`.
   - D (2094): pointer on the note body (`index >= 0`) → `selectOnly` + `beginMove` → move.
   - E/F: empty space dismisses the selection box, or paints a new note.
3. **Grab zone (`edgeGrabAt`, PREW:1422–1451):** it walks the active track's notes at the pointer's pitch in reverse order. It measures in pixels, `x0 = xOfStep(start)` and `x1 = xOfStep(end())`, and sets `zone = min(EDGE_GRAB_W, max(1, (x1-x0)/3))` (PREW:1439).
   - `EDGE_GRAB_W` is 4 px (`src/modules/PianoRoll/PianoRollGeometry.hpp:127`).
   - So the zone is 4 px either side of the edge for notes 12 px or wider. Below that it shrinks to a third of the width, never less than 1 px, so a narrow note isn't swallowed whole and can still be moved.
4. **Right edge is tested first** (PREW:1441): `|x - x1| <= zone` returns at once with `left_edge = false`. It wins any overlap with the left zone.
5. **Why a short note's left edge can't be grabbed** (PREW:1445): the left test also needs `(x1-x0) > EDGE_GRAB_MIN_NOTE_W`. That constant is `EDGE_GRAB_W * 2.5` = 10 px (`src/modules/PianoRoll/PianoRollGeometry.hpp:128`). A note 10 px wide or narrower has no left grab zone at all, so a left grab can't be confused with a right one (comment at PREW:1443). A press there falls through to D and moves the note.
6. **Edge vs body:** `noteIndexAt` uses the half-open span `[start, end)`. The right edge is only reachable because `edgeGrabAt` tests in pixels, including pixels just past `end` (comment at PREW:1400–1404).
7. **Cursor feedback:** `onHover` (PREW:1595–1599) follows the same order (box → edge → body) to show the move, resize or hand cursor. It can't see modifier keys, so a Shift-click still shows the plain cursor.
