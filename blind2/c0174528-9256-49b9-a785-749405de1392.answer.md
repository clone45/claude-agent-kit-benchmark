Everything below is in `src/modules/PianoRoll/PianoRollEditorWidget.hpp` unless another file is named.

1. **Entry.** `onButton` (l.1624) sends a left press to `beginDrag(pos, mods)` (l.1889). That calls `zoneAt` (l.1387), and if the result is `ZONE_GRID` it calls `beginGridDrag(position, shift)` (l.2032).
2. **Order in `beginGridDrag`.** It first gets the note under the pointer from `noteIndexAt(stepAtX, pitchAtY)` (l.1405). It then tries each step in turn:
   - (A) Shift held: adds or removes the note from the selection, or starts a marquee.
   - (B) Press inside the multi-note selection box from `selectionBounds` (l.1453): moves the group. Edges are never tested here, so notes in a group can't be resized (l.2060–2074).
   - (C) `edgeGrabAt(position, grab)` (l.1422) finds an edge: `DRAG_RESIZE` starts, and `drag_left_edge = grab.left_edge` picks which edge (l.2077–2091).
   - (D) A note body was hit: `selectOnly` + `beginMove`. (E) Clears the selection. (F) Paints a new note.
   - `onHover` (l.1590–1601) runs the same box → edge → body order to choose the cursor.
3. **The grab zone (`edgeGrabAt`, l.1422–1451).** It walks the active track's notes at the pointer's pitch, newest first, and works in pixels: `x0 = xOfStep(start)`, `x1 = xOfStep(end())`. The zone is `min(EDGE_GRAB_W, max(1, (x1-x0)/3))` (l.1439), measured on both sides of each edge. `EDGE_GRAB_W` is 4 px (`PianoRollGeometry.hpp:127`). So notes 12 px or wider get the full ±4 px. Narrower notes get a third of their width, at least 1 px, so the zones never cover the whole note and the body can still be grabbed to move it.
4. **Right edge first.** The right edge is tested first (l.1441). Because the zone reaches past `x1`, the end can be grabbed even though `noteIndexAt` uses a half-open span that leaves the end step outside the note (comment at l.1400–1404).
5. **Why a short note's left edge can't be grabbed.** The left-edge test (l.1445) also needs `(x1-x0) > EDGE_GRAB_MIN_NOTE_W`. That constant is `EDGE_GRAB_W * 2.5` = 10 px (`PianoRollGeometry.hpp:128`). On a note 10 px wide or less, a left-edge grab could be mistaken for a right-edge grab, so it is turned off. A press there falls through to (D) and moves the note. Only the right edge can resize it.
