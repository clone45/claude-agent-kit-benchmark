`Sentinel` in `src/click/_utils.py` is an `enum.Enum` whose members, such as `UNSET`, are meant to be singletons. Copying a member with `copy.copy` or `copy.deepcopy`, or round-tripping it through `pickle`, must return the very same member object. Today the default Enum reduction uses the member's value, which is a bare `object()`, so the round trip either fails or yields a different object.

Make `copy.copy(s) is s`, `copy.deepcopy(s) is s`, and `pickle.loads(pickle.dumps(s)) is s` hold for every `Sentinel` member. A consequence that must also hold: a `click.Option(["--name"])`, whose `default` is `UNSET`, still has `default is UNSET` and `name == "name"` after being copied, deep-copied, or pickled and unpickled. Add tests.
